A little more about me

I’m a Computer Science student at Worcester Polytechnic Institute with minors in Entrepreneurship and Management Information Systems, passionate about building technology that is both technically thoughtful and meaningful to the people who use it.

Through my academic, personal, and professional experiences, I’ve explored software engineering, front-end development, product thinking, and user-centered design. I have had the opportunity to work across Product Management and Software Engineering — conducting research, shaping product requirements, designing user experiences, navigating an existing codebase, and supporting implementation of a customer-facing experience.

Outside of internships, I enjoy building projects that challenge me to think about both the technical and human sides of software. Whether I’m creating interactive data experiences, developing web applications, or exploring new technologies, I’m especially interested in how thoughtful design and engineering can make complex information and experiences more accessible.

I’m driven by continuous learning, collaboration, and growth, and I’m excited to keep developing as an engineer, product thinker, and leader while building products with the people who use them in mind.
